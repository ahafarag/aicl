Would an AI detector flag your writing?

In a 2023 Stanford study, seven AI detectors wrongly flagged 61% of essays by non-native English writers on average. Paste your text to see the signals detectors react to, measured openly and compared with real human and GPT essays.

🔒 Runs entirely in your browser. Your text never leaves your device.

The first analysis downloads about 415 MB of language models, which your browser then caches. Wi-Fi recommended.

Open source: github.com/ahafarag/aicl · methods, data and the full detector report card. Reference data: Liang et al. (2023), GPT detectors are biased against non-native English writers, Patterns 4(7), MIT licence.
